## Why Not Smart Contract Chain


Smart contract chain takes all computation and storage on the same chain.

Even though L2, use the same strategy, which leads yet expensive computation and storage cost. L2 is not the solution for internet-rank decentralized application.

Modular blockchains can only improve the L1/L2's capabilities, not able to solve the problems of real world usecase applications.

So we believe the smart contract chain is NOT the right choice to accomodate the real world application.
