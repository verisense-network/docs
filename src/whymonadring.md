## Why Monadring


If the `nucleus` network still adopts traditional BFT-like consensus protocol, 

two problems:

1. hard to reduce the network overload;
2. hard to minimize the network size while keeping secure.

Monadring is the protocol introduced by Verisense team dedicate to overcome the above problems.

It has features:

1. potential to high security in small network;
2. high throughout in small network;
